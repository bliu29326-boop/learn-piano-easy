# 0015: The difficulty is five chips, and every song without versions of ours is an Original

## Decision

- The song explorer's difficulty is five chips: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard and Original.
- A level is one of four bands of the catalog's number. Beginner is below 2, Easy below 3 and Medium
  below 4. Hard starts at 4.
- Every catalog row without a version of ours is an Original. That is a composer's score, a piece of
  ours with one version, or our Original of a song.
- An Original carries the Original chip and the band its number lies in.
- The five chips are one kind of tag, so two chips widen the result.
- Every catalog card shows its versions as steps. A card with one version shows one step, Original.
- A card shows its difficulty by its steps alone. It has no level word and no meter.

This replaces the six levels, the meter and the absent filter by version of decision 0009. Its cards,
its names and its ids stay.

## Why

The user found no way to filter the Originals. The catalog holds one Original for each of 386 of its
461 songs, so the filter is the way to find the composers' own scores.

The user wanted one form for a difficulty. A card of ours named it by its steps, and a card of the
catalog by a level word and a meter. Two forms read as two scales, and the meter repeated the word.

Advanced and Expert named no version. Every version of ours lies in the first four bands, so the two
levels above them served the catalog's scores alone. Their scores now read as Originals in Hard.

## Consequences

- "Hard" keeps our hard versions and every catalog score from 4 up, so it holds most of the catalog.
- "Original" with a band widens to both, since the chips are one kind. Easy originals are found by
  "Easy" alone, and their cards show the Original step.
- A score of the catalog shows no difficulty on its card. Its band is read through the chips.
- The texts of Advanced and Expert leave every locale.
- **Performance:** each row carries one more tag. A card draws one element fewer.
- **Memory:** one more tag per Original, which is a few hundred set entries.
- **Usability:** the learner reads one scale on every card and in the filter. A hard Original no longer
  tells its learner how hard it is beyond Hard.
- **Simplicity:** the levels shrink from six to four, and the meter and the level word leave the card.

## Rejected

- **Original as a kind of its own, beside the difficulty:** two chips would then narrow each other. The
  user wanted the five chips as one form.
- **Keeping Advanced and Expert for the catalog's scores:** two levels no version of ours has made a
  second scale on the same row.
- **Showing the band beside the Original step:** it would be a second form of the difficulty on a card.
